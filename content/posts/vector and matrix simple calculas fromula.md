$\mathbf{x}$ is a vector of n dims, $\nabla$ is the nabla operator

$$ for A \in \mathbf{R}^{m\times{n}}, \nabla{\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}} = \mathbf{A}^{T} $$ 
$$ for A \in \mathbf{R}^{n\times{m}},  \nabla{\mathbf{x}^{T}\mathbf{A}} = \mathbf{A} $$
$$ for A \in \mathbf{R}^{n\times{n}},  \nabla{\mathbf{x}^{T}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}} = (\mathbf{A} +  \mathbf{A}^{T})\mathbf{x} $$
$$ \nabla{||\mathbf{x}||^2} = \nabla{\mathbf{x}^{T}\mathbf{x}} = 2\mathbf{x} $$
